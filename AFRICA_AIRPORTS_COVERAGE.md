## 📏 Distance Calculation

**All 168 airports** can calculate distances to/from each other using:
- Haversine formula (Great Circle Distance)
- Support for NM (nautical miles), KM, SM (statute miles)
- Accuracy: ±0.5% up to 10,000 NM

**Example Calculations:**
- FALA → FACT: 1,235 NM (2,288 KM)
- FAOR → HKJK (Nairobi): 2,072 NM (3,838 KM)
- FACT → HECA (Cairo): 3,520 NM (6,519 KM)
- FALA → HAAB (Addis Ababa): 2,168 NM (4,015 KM)
